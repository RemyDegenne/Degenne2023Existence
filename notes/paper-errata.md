# Errata of *On the Existence of a Complexity in Fixed Budget Bandit Identification*

Errors found in the paper while formalizing it, with their corrections. Each entry gives where the
error is (the revision of the paper, the result, the page or equation), the error, the correction,
the declarations that state the corrected form, the kind of error and when it was found. Entries
are numbered `E1`, `E2`, … and never renumbered.

The paper is R. Degenne, COLT 2023, PMLR 195, arXiv:2303.09468v2; pages are those of the arXiv PDF.
Arms are numbered from `1` in the paper and from `0` in Lean.

## E1. Theorem 1: the arbitrary extension of `i*` outside `𝒟` breaks the rate of static proportions

- **Where:** arXiv:2303.09468v2, Section 1, "Assumptions on the identification problem", page 3
  ("We then extend `i*` beyond `𝒟` … by giving it an arbitrary value outside of `𝒟`"); Theorem 1,
  page 6, and its consequence `H_{𝒞^{sp}}(μ) = (max_ω inf_{λ ∈ Alt(μ)} ∑_k ω_k KL(λ_k, μ_k))⁻¹`;
  proof in Appendix A, page 16; Theorem 5, page 9, which uses Theorem 1.
- **Error:** a static proportions algorithm recommends `i*(μ̂_T)`, and `μ̂_T` can take values in
  `cl(𝒟) \ 𝒟` with positive probability. The proof of Theorem 1 bounds the probability of error by
  that of `μ̂_T ∉ 𝒟_{i*(μ)}` and identifies the infimum of the rate function over the closure of
  that set with its infimum over `Alt(μ)`. This holds only if the error set
  `{x : i*(x) ≠ i*(μ)}` stays within `cl(Alt(μ))`, which an arbitrary extension does not ensure.
  Counterexample: best arm identification with two Bernoulli arms, `𝒟 = {μ ∈ (0, 1)² : μ₁ ≠ μ₂}`,
  extension `i*(1, 0) = 2`, `μ = (0.99, 0.01)`, `ω = (1/2, 1/2)`. The empirical means equal `(1, 0)`
  when all samples of arm 1 are `1` and all samples of arm 2 are `0`, with probability
  `0.99^{N_{T,1}} 0.99^{N_{T,2}} = 0.99^T`, so `p_{μ,T} ≥ 0.99^T` and
  `liminf_T h_{μ,T} ≥ 1 / log(1/0.99) ≈ 99.5`, while the formula of Theorem 1 gives
  `(min_x (KL(x, 0.99) + KL(x, 0.01)) / 2)⁻¹ = 1 / KL(1/2, 0.99) ≈ 0.62`. The point `(1, 0)` is a
  limit of `𝒟_1` only. For the same reason the displayed value of `H_{𝒞^{sp}}` fails for such an
  extension (every static proportions algorithm recommends `i*(1, 0)` there), and Theorem 5 fails
  for the algorithms using it.
- **Correction:** assume that the extension answers `i` at every point `x` of the closure of the
  vectors of means of the families that is not a limit of alternatives to `i`
  (`x ∉ cl(Alt_i)`); under Assumption 1 with finitely many answers such an extension exists
  (answer some `i` with `x ∈ cl(𝒟_i)`, which is unique when `x` is a limit of a single `𝒟_i`), and
  with it the proof of Theorem 1 goes through: the error set within the closure of the vectors of
  means lies in `cl(Alt(μ))`. The class `𝒞^{sp}` can be taken to contain the static proportions
  algorithms with every extension correct on `𝒟`: its oracle difficulty is then the displayed
  value, as the algorithms with a good extension attain it and every algorithm of the class errs
  at least when `μ̂_T ∈ Alt(μ)`.
- **Lean:** `Degenne2023Existence.tendsto_errorRate_of_isStaticProportions` and
  `Degenne2023Existence.maxRatio_staticDifficulty_le_of_isStaticProportions` (and
  `maxRatio_staticDifficulty_le_card`) assume the condition on the recommendation rule `rec`
  (hypothesis `hrec`); `LMLExplore.Identification.IdentTask.staticClass` contains the static
  proportions families with every recommendation rule correct on the admissible models, and
  `Degenne2023Existence.oracleDifficulty_staticClass` states the displayed value for it.
- **Kind:** false statement (missing hypothesis on the extension of `i*`).
- **Found:** phase-1 statement check, 2026-11-09.

## E2. Section 1: the empirical means need not lie in the closure of the means of the family

- **Where:** arXiv:2303.09468v2, Section 1, "Assumptions on the identification problem", page 3
  ("The empirical mean `μ̂_{T,k} ∈ cl(ℳ_k)` of an arm `k` is the maximum likelihood estimator");
  used in the proof of Theorem 1 (Appendix A, page 16) and in the remark after Assumption 1 that the
  empirical means are always in the closure of `𝒟`.
- **Error:** for a canonical one-parameter exponential family that is not steep, the samples, hence
  the empirical means, can lie outside the closure of the interval of means. Example: the family
  generated by the density `e^{-x} / (1 + x³)` on `[0, ∞)`, whose natural parameters are `η ≤ 1`;
  its means are `(0, 1)` (at `η = 1` the mean is `∫ x / (1 + x³) / ∫ 1 / (1 + x³) = 1`), while every
  sample exceeds `1` with positive probability. The empirical means then take values where `i*` is
  arbitrary and where the rate function is finite, as in E1.
- **Correction:** assume that the samples lie in the closure of the means, `ρ(cl(ℳ_k)ᶜ) = 0` for the
  base measure `ρ` of each family (true for steep families: Gaussian, Bernoulli, Poisson,
  exponential, …).
- **Lean:** `Degenne2023Existence.tendsto_errorRate_of_isStaticProportions`,
  `Degenne2023Existence.oracleDifficulty_staticClass` and the two results of Theorem 5
  assume `hρ_closure : ∀ a, ∀ᵐ x ∂(ρ a), x ∈ closure ((ρ a).expFamilyMeans id)`.
- **Kind:** missing hypothesis.
- **Found:** phase-1 statement check, 2026-11-09.

## E3. Theorem 6: the KL ratio does not tend to `1` as `ℓ` tends to the lower end of the means

- **Where:** arXiv:2303.09468v2, proof of Theorem 6, page 10 ("When `ℓ` tends to the lower bound of
  the means in the exponential family, the KL ratio tends to 1").
- **Error:** the lower bound of the proof is `K KL(θ, ℓ) / KL(m, ℓ)`. For Bernoulli arms,
  `KL(θ, ℓ) ~ θ log(1/ℓ)` and `KL(m, ℓ) ~ m log(1/ℓ)` as `ℓ → 0`, so the ratio tends to `θ / m < 1`;
  the same happens for Poisson and exponential arms. The limit is `1` for Gaussian arms.
- **Correction:** fix `ℓ < θ` and let `m ↓ θ`: by continuity of `KL(·, ℓ)` on the means and
  `KL(θ, ℓ) > 0`, the ratio tends to `1`, so the infimum is at least `K`. The statement of
  Theorem 6 is unaffected.
- **Lean:** `Degenne2023Existence.iInf_maxRatio_minThresholdTask_eq_card` states Theorem 6 as
  printed (with the threshold a mean of every family); the proof follows the correction.
- **Kind:** gap in the proof.
- **Found:** phase-1 statement check, 2026-11-09.

## E4. Lemmas 17 and 18: misprinted statements

- **Where:** arXiv:2303.09468v2, Appendix C, Lemmas 17 and 18, page 19.
- **Error:** Lemma 17 is printed without the weights `σ_k⁻²`,
  `max_ω inf_{λ ∈ Alt(μ) ∩ B(η, r)} ∑_k ω_k (λ_k - μ_k)² = ((μ - η)ᵀ u)²`, which is false for
  variances other than `1` under the normalization `‖u · σ‖₁ = 1`; its proof uses
  `∑_k ω_k σ_k⁻² (λ_k - μ_k)²`, the setting of the section. In the proof, the point
  `λ_u(μ) = μ - ((μ - η)ᵀ u) σ` is on the hyperplane only if `σᵀ u = ‖u · σ‖₁`, that is for `u ≥ 0`;
  in general the direction is `(sign(u_k) σ_k)_k`. Lemma 18 is printed as
  `max_ω inf_{λ ∈ D_{ε,δ}(μ)} ∑_k ω_k KL(λ_k, μ_k) ≤ (1 + ε)(1 + δ)²`, while its proof (and its use in
  Theorem 7 through Theorem 3) bounds `max_ω inf_{λ ∈ D_{ε,δ}(μ)} H_{𝒞^{sp}}(λ) ∑_k ω_k KL(μ_k, λ_k)`.
- **Correction:** Lemma 17 with the weights `σ_k⁻²` and the direction `(sign(u_k) σ_k)_k`; Lemma 18
  for the quantity of its proof, with `2((λ - η)ᵀ u)⁻²` in place of `H_{𝒞^{sp}}(λ)` (see E5).
- **Lean:** the Lean proof of Theorem 7, `Degenne2023Existence.one_le_iInf_maxRatio_of_hyperplane`,
  does not go through Lemmas 17 and 18: it bounds `H_{𝒞^{sp}}` near `η` directly (E5) and uses
  the alternatives `μ - t ((μ - η)ᵀ u) d` with the direction `d_k = sign(u_k) σ_k / ‖u · σ‖₁`
  (`LMLExplore.Identification.exists_dotProduct_eq_one_sum_le`). Lemmas 17 and 18 remain blueprint
  statements in the corrected forms (`lem:ball_half_space_difficulty`, `lem:ball_bound`).
- **Kind:** typo.
- **Found:** phase-1 statement check, 2026-11-09.

## E5. Lemma 18: the value of `H_{𝒞^{sp}}` near the hyperplane

- **Where:** arXiv:2303.09468v2, proof of Lemma 18, page 20 ("For all `λ ∈ Alt(μ) ∩ B(η, r')`,
  Lemma 17 gives `H_{𝒞^{sp}}(λ) = 2((λ - η)ᵀ u)⁻²`").
- **Error:** Lemma 17 computes the infimum over the alternatives within the ball `B(η, r)`, while
  `H_{𝒞^{sp}}(λ)⁻¹` takes the infimum over all of `Alt(λ)`; alternatives outside the ball can be
  cheaper for some allocations, and then `H_{𝒞^{sp}}(λ) > 2((λ - η)ᵀ u)⁻²`. Counterexample: two
  Gaussian arms of variance `1`, `η = 0`, `u = (1, 0)`, `r = 1`, and three answers
  `𝒟_1 = {λ₁ < 0, λ₂ < 3/2}`, `𝒟_2 = {λ₁ > 0, λ₂ < 3/2}`, `𝒟_3 = {λ₂ > 3/2}` (Assumption 1 holds, and
  the ball meets only `𝒟_1`, `𝒟_2` and the hyperplane). For `λ = (-d, 0)` with `0 < d < r'`,
  `H_{𝒞^{sp}}(λ)⁻¹ = max_ω min(ω₁ d², ω₂ (3/2)²) / 2 = d² (9/4) / (2 (d² + 9/4)) < d² / 2`.
- **Correction:** for allocations with all coordinates at least `ε`, the alternatives outside
  `B(η, r)` cost at least `ε (r - ‖λ - η‖)² / (2 max_k σ_k²)`, which is large compared to
  `((λ - η)ᵀ u)²` when `λ` is close to the hyperplane; the `ω^ε` perturbation of the proof then gives
  `H_{𝒞^{sp}}(λ) ≤ (1 + ε') 2((λ - η)ᵀ u)⁻²` for the alternatives used, `λ_{ω^ε}(μ)`, whose distance
  to the hyperplane is `x`, with `ε' → 0` as `x → 0`. Theorem 7 follows as in the paper.
- **Lean:** `Degenne2023Existence.one_le_iInf_maxRatio_of_hyperplane` states and proves Theorem 7
  as printed; `Degenne2023Existence.exists_ofReal_le_inv_staticDifficulty_of_hyperplane`
  (blueprint `lem:static_near_hyperplane`) carries the correction: for every `c ∈ (0, 1)`, near
  `η`, `H_{𝒞^{sp}}(λ)⁻¹ ≥ c ((λ - η)ᵀ u)² / (2 ‖u · σ‖₁²)`, with the allocation
  `c ω* + (1 - c)/K`.
- **Kind:** gap in the proof.
- **Found:** phase-1 statement check, 2026-11-09.

## E6. Theorem 8: the last inequality of the proof fails for `K = 2`

- **Where:** arXiv:2303.09468v2, proof of Theorem 8, Appendix D, page 22
  (`(1/8) ∑_{j=2}^K 1/j ≥ (1/8)(log(K + 1) - log 2) ≥ (3/40) log K`).
- **Error:** `(log 3 - log 2) / 8 ≈ 0.0507 < (3/40) log 2 ≈ 0.0520`, so the second inequality fails
  for `K = 2` (it holds for `K ≥ 3`, since `log((K + 1)/2) - (3/5) log K` is increasing for
  `K ≥ 2` and positive at `K = 3`).
- **Correction:** `∑_{j=2}^K 1/j ≥ (3/5) log K` for all `K ≥ 1` (at `K = 2`, `1/2 ≥ 0.416`), so
  `(1/8) ∑_{j=2}^K 1/j ≥ (3/40) log K` and the statement of Theorem 8 holds.
- **Lean:** `Degenne2023Existence.ofReal_mul_log_le_iInf_maxRatio_baiTask_gaussian` states
  Theorem 8 as printed.
- **Kind:** wrong constant (in the proof).
- **Found:** phase-1 statement check, 2026-11-09.

## E7. Theorem 10: the range of `x`

- **Where:** arXiv:2303.09468v2, proof of Theorem 10, Appendix D, page 24 ("For `x ∈ (0, 1/2)`").
- **Error:** `λ⁽²⁾(x) = (x(1 + x), 1/2)` is an alternative to `μ(x) = (x(1 + x), x)` only if
  `x(1 + x) < 1/2`, that is `x < (√3 - 1)/2 ≈ 0.366`.
- **Correction:** take `x ∈ (0, (√3 - 1)/2)`; the proof uses `x → 0` only.
- **Lean:** `Degenne2023Existence.one_lt_iInf_maxRatio_baiTask_bernoulli_two` states
  Theorem 10 as printed.
- **Kind:** typo.
- **Found:** phase-1 statement check, 2026-11-09.
