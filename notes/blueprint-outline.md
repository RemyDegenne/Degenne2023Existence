# Blueprint outline: [D23]

R. Degenne, *On the Existence of a Complexity in Fixed Budget Bandit Identification*, COLT 2023,
PMLR 195, arXiv:2303.09468v2 (`source/arXiv-2303.09468v2/`). Theorems, lemmas and corollaries
share one counter (Theorem 1, …, Lemma 20); the single assumption is Assumption 1. Main text:
Theorems 1–3, Corollary 4, Theorems 5–8, Lemma 9, Theorem 10. Appendix: Lemma 11, Theorem 12,
Theorem 13, Corollary 14, Lemmas 15–20.

This file is the contract between the blueprint chapters and the Lean files: it fixes the labels,
the modelling and the proof routes. The paper is also the subject of the LMLRoadmaps target
Identification, Layer 8.3 (large deviations under static allocations, [D23, Theorems 1, 12]) and
Layer 8.4 (the results of [D23] on the existence of a complexity).

## 1. Modelling

**Tasks.** LMLExplore's identification tasks `IdentTask α Θ ℐ 𝓨` (`LMLExplore/Identification/Task`):
admissible models `task.M` (the paper's `𝒟`), bandits `task.ν θ : Kernel α 𝓨`, correct answers
`task.correct θ`, answer sets `task.answerSet i` (`𝒟_i`), alternatives `task.alt i`. The general
results (Theorems 2, 3, 13, Corollaries 4, 14) are stated for every task, with any models `Θ` and
observations `𝓨`. The others are stated for tasks on the vectors of means `μ : α → ℝ` with real
observations, the arm `a` in the exponential family generated by `ρ a : Measure ℝ` with the
statistic `id`: `task.ν μ a = (ρ a).expFamily id ((ρ a).natParam id (μ a))` for the means
`μ a ∈ (ρ a).expFamilyMeans id`, the pattern of the GK16 project (only on the means, so that the
hypothesis can hold for families whose natural parameters are bounded). The classical tasks are
LMLExplore's: positivity is `minThresholdTask` (answer `true` iff some mean is below `γ`), the
half-space task is `halfSpaceTask`, best arm identification is `baiTask`, restricted to the vectors
of means of the families; Gaussian arms are `gaussianReal (μ a) (v a)` (per-arm variances) or
`Bandits.gaussianBandit · 1`, Bernoulli arms `Bandits.bernoulliBandit` of `projIcc 0 1 (μ c)` on
`{μ | ∀ c, μ c ∈ Ioo 0 1}`, as in `LMLExplore/Identification/EpsBestArmLowerBound.lean`.

**Alternatives.** `IdentTask.alternatives task θ`: the admissible models with no correct answer in
common with `θ`, the paper's `Alt(θ)` for single-answer tasks. Lower bounds use the disjointness
of the correct answers instead of `i*(λ) ≠ i*(μ)`.

**Algorithm families.** Unbundled: `𝒜 : ℕ → IdentAlg Unit α 𝓨 ℐ` (LML's identification
algorithms) with `IsFixedBudgetFamily 𝒜 := ∀ T, IsFixedBudget (𝒜 T) T` (LMLExplore's
`Identification/FixedBudget.lean`). Classes are sets of families; `𝒞_∞` is
`{𝒜 | IsFixedBudgetFamily 𝒜}`, and `inf_{𝒜 ∈ 𝒞_∞}` is `⨅ 𝒜 (_ : IsFixedBudgetFamily 𝒜)`.

**Rates, in `ℝ≥0∞`.** `errorProb task 𝒜 θ T = p_{θ,T}(𝒜)`, the mass of `(task.correct θ)ᶜ` under
`(𝒜 T).outputMeasure (Environment.bandit (task.ν θ))`; `errorRate = h = T / (-log p)` with
`ENNReal.log`, so `h = 0` at `p = 0` and `h = ∞` at `p = 1`, `T > 0`; `oracleDifficulty task 𝒞 θ =
H_𝒞(θ) = ⨅ 𝒜 ∈ 𝒞, limsup_T h`; `difficultyRatio H 𝒜 θ T = h / H θ`, `asymptoticRatio = limsup_T`,
`maxRatio H 𝒜 = ⨆ θ ∈ M, asymptoticRatio`; `IsConsistent`: `p → 0` on `M`. Difficulty functions
`H : Θ → ℝ≥0∞`; the results assume `H ≠ 0` and `H ≠ ∞` where the paper's `H : 𝒟 → ℝ⁺` is used.

**Static proportions.** `IsStaticProportions task rec 𝒜 w`: `w` positive summing to `1`; every
`𝒜 T` fixed-budget with `|N_{T,a} - T w a| ≤ K` almost surely under the trajectory law on every
admissible model, and output rule `outputAt (𝒜 T) T h = dirac (rec (empMean' T h))`.
`staticClass task` (`𝒞^{sp}`): static proportions families with some `w` and some `rec` correct on
`M`. The paper fixes one arbitrary extension `i*` of the answer outside `𝒟`; Theorem 1 is false for
some extensions (E1), so the class takes every extension (its oracle difficulty is unchanged), and
the per-algorithm results (Theorems 1, 5) assume that `rec x = i` at the points `x` of the closure
of the vectors of means that are not limits of alternatives to `i`.

**`H_{𝒞^{sp}}`.** `staticDifficulty task θ = (⨆ w ∈ simplex α, ⨅ λ ∈ alternatives θ, task.cost w λ θ)⁻¹`,
with LMLExplore's transportation cost `cost w λ θ = ∑ a, w a KL(ν_a(λ), ν_a(θ))` (alternative first).
Theorem 1 identifies it with `oracleDifficulty task staticClass`. Statements about `H_{𝒞^{sp}}`
(Theorems 5, 6, 7, Lemmas 9, 15, 16, 19, 20) use `staticDifficulty`, so that they do not depend on
the large deviations of Theorem 1; statements quantifying over the classes `𝒞 ⊇ 𝒞^{sp}` (Theorems 8,
10) use `oracleDifficulty task 𝒞`.

**Large deviations.** `IsLDP P I` (speed `n`, `I : X → ℝ≥0∞` lower semicontinuous, the bounds
`-inf_{int s} I ≤ liminf (1/n) log P n s` and `limsup (1/n) log P n s ≤ -inf_{cl s} I` for every
set, `EReal` logarithms) and `IsGoodRateFunction`, in
`ForLMLExplore/Probability/LargeDeviations/Basic.lean`; no such notion exists in Mathlib, Tau Ceti,
LML or LMLExplore. The rate of an arm is `(ρ a).expFamilyRate id x (μ a)`, the Cramér rate function
of the member, equal to `KL(x, μ a)` on the means.

**Assumption 1** is `IsRegular task S` (`D2023/Setting.lean`), with
`S = {μ | ∀ a, μ a ∈ (ρ a).expFamilyMeans id}`. The paper also uses that the empirical means lie in
the closure of the means (E2): hypothesis `hρ_closure : ∀ a, ∀ᵐ x ∂(ρ a), x ∈ closure (means)`.

## 3. Proof routes

Part II items (library-shaped, `ForLMLExplore/`) have labels `def:…`/`lem:…` below; Part I follows
the paper.

### Chapter `setting` (Sections 1–2)
Definitions `def:fixed_budget_family`, `def:alternatives`, `def:error_prob`, `def:error_rate`,
`def:oracle_difficulty`, `def:difficulty_ratio`, `def:consistent`, `def:static_proportions`,
`def:static_class`, `def:static_difficulty`, `asm:answer_nhds`.

### Chapter `static` (Theorem 1, Lemma 11, Theorem 12; Section 2, Appendix A)
- Lemma 11: Cramér's theorem for the member of mean `μ` (`lem:cramer`), with the goodness of its
  rate function (`lem:rate_good`). Upper bound: LMLExplore's Cramér–Chernoff bounds
  (`Probability/LargeDeviations/Chernoff.lean`, `ExponentialFamily/Chernoff.lean`); lower bound:
  `ExponentialFamily/Cramer.lean` at the means, and the levels of finite rate outside the means
  (boundary atoms, as Bernoulli `0` and `1`) by a direct bound.
- Theorem 12: in the stack-of-rewards model (LML `RewardByCountMeasure`), the empirical mean of arm
  `a` is the mean of the first `N_{T,a}` samples of its stack, with `N_{T,a}` within `K` of `T w a`;
  LDP for the vector of independent per-arm means with speeds `⌊T w a⌋` (`lem:ldp_pi`), robust to the
  window of counts (`lem:ldp_window`), and the scaling of the speed (`lem:ldp_speed`).
- Theorem 1: Theorem 12; the error event lies between `{μ̂ ∈ Alt(μ)}` (open: `IsRegular`,
  single answer) and `{μ̂ ∈ cl S, rec μ̂ ≠ i}`, contained in `cl Alt(μ)` by `hrec`
  (`lem:error_set_subset`); the rate is continuous on `cl S` (`lem:rate_continuousOn`) so its
  infimum over `cl Alt(μ)` is the one over `Alt(μ)`; `lem:tendsto_errorRate_iff` converts
  `(1/T) log p → -c` into `h → c⁻¹`.
- Theorem 1 (display): lower bound for every member of `staticClass` (any `rec` correct on `M`
  errs on `{μ̂ ∈ Alt(μ)}`), upper bound from a static proportions family with a good `rec` for every
  `w ∈ Δ_K^0` (`lem:exists_static`: cumulative tracking with constant targets,
  `Identification/CumulativeTracking.lean`, `|N - T w| ≤ K - 1`, and `fixedBudget`; `lem:exists_rec`:
  a measurable good recommendation rule under Assumption 1), and `sup_{Δ_K^0} = sup_{Δ_K}` by
  concavity (`lem:iSup_open_simplex`, LMLExplore's `concaveOn_altCost` pattern for the reversed
  cost). The upper half alone (`lem:oracleDifficulty_staticClass_le`) is what Theorems 8 and 10 use.

### Chapter `lower` (Theorems 2, 3, Corollary 4, Theorem 13, Corollary 14; Section 3, Appendix B)
- Theorem 2: the change of measure at the budget, `kl(P_μ(E), P_λ(E)) ≤ ∑ a, E_μ[N_{T,a}] KL(ν_a(μ),
  ν_a(λ))` for the event `E` that the output is correct for `μ` (`lem:fb_change_of_measure`, from
  LMLExplore's `Identification/ChangeOfMeasure.lean` and `IsFixedBudget`), `kl(a, b) ≥ a log (1/b) -
  log 2` (`lem:klBer_ge`), and `R_{H,T}(𝒜, λ)⁻¹ = H(λ) log(1/p_{λ,T}) / T` (`lem:inv_difficultyRatio`).
- Theorem 3: Theorem 2 along a subsequence on which `E_μ[N_T / T]` converges in the compact simplex
  (`lem:tendsto_subseq_proportions`), and `(limsup R)⁻¹ = liminf R⁻¹`. Second part: `D = Alt(μ)`
  and an infimum over `μ`.
- Corollary 4: Theorem 3 with `D = {λ j | j ∈ s}`, and `max_w min_{j ∈ s} c_j w_j = (∑_{j ∈ s} c_j⁻¹)⁻¹`
  (`lem:iSup_iInf_mul`, a subset version of LMLExplore's `answerValue_eq_of_altCost_eq_iInf`).
- Theorem 13: Theorem 2 for all `λ ∈ D` at once (`H` bounded), and the limit. Corollary 14:
  Theorem 13 on `Alt_x(μ)`.
- `lem:asymptoticRatio_eq_top`: if `p_{μ,T} ↛ 0` at `μ ∈ M` with `H(μ) < ∞`, then
  `R_{H,∞}(𝒜, μ) = ∞`; used in Theorems 6, 7, 8, 10 and Lemma 16 for the families that are not
  consistent at the chosen model.

### Chapter `range` (Theorems 5–7, Lemmas 15–18; Section 4, Appendix C)
- Theorem 5: Theorem 1 and `w ≥ (min_j w_j) w'` for `w'` in the simplex (monotonicity of the cost).
- Theorem 6: `≤` for a uniform static proportions family (`lem:exists_static`) recommending
  `decide (∃ a, x a < γ)`: Theorem 12 and the monotonicity of the rates on each side of the means
  give its exponent on both answers without the closure hypothesis of Theorem 5 (or Theorem 5,
  with `lem:isRegular_minThresholdTask`, for steep families); `≥` by Corollary 4 at `μ = (m, …, m)`, `m > γ`, with
  `λ⁽ʲ⁾ = μ` but `ℓ < γ` at `j`, `H^{sp}(λ⁽ʲ⁾) = KL(γ, ℓ)⁻¹` (`lem:static_minThreshold_one_low`,
  from LMLExplore's `Task/Threshold.lean` for the reversed cost), the bound `K KL(γ, ℓ) / KL(m, ℓ)`,
  and `m ↓ γ` (E3).
- Lemma 15: the infimum over the half-space of `∑ w_a (λ_a - μ_a)² / (2 v_a)` is
  `(u ⬝ μ)² / (2 ∑ u_a² v_a / w_a)` (LMLExplore `Task/HalfSpace.lean` for a common variance), and
  Cauchy–Schwarz for the maximum over `w`.
- Lemma 16: Theorem 3 (second part), Lemma 15, and `max_w inf_{a > 0} (√a + |u ⬝ ν|)² / (a ‖u‖²) = 1`.
- Lemma 17 (`lem:ball_half_space_difficulty`), Lemma 18 (`lem:ball_bound`): the computations of the
  appendix with the weights `σ_a⁻²` (E4); Lemma 18 with `2 ((λ - η) ⬝ u)⁻²` in place of
  `H^{sp}(λ)`.
- `lem:static_near_hyperplane`: for every `ε > 0`, `H^{sp}(λ) ≤ (1 + ε) 2 ((λ - η) ⬝ u)⁻²` for the
  alternatives `λ` close enough to `η`, with the alternatives outside the ball costing at least
  `min_a w_a (r - ‖λ - η‖)² / (2 max_a v_a)` (the fix of E5).
- Theorem 7: Theorem 3 (first part) with `D(μ) = Alt(μ) ∩ B(η, r')`, Lemma 18 and
  `lem:static_near_hyperplane`, then `ε, δ → 0`.

### Chapter `bai` (Theorems 8, 10, Lemmas 9, 19, 20; Section 5, Appendix D)
- Theorem 8: `H_𝒞 ≤ H_{𝒞^{sp}} ≤ H^{sp}` (`lem:oracleDifficulty_mono`,
  `lem:oracleDifficulty_staticClass_le`, `lem:isRegular_baiTask`), `H^{sp} = T*` for Gaussian arms
  (symmetric divergence, `lem:staticDifficulty_eq_charTime`), `T* ≤ 2 H_Δ` (LMLExplore
  `charTime_baiTask_gaussianBandit_le`), Corollary 4 at `μ_k = -k Δ` with the arms `k ≥ 2`,
  `H_Δ(λ⁽ʲ⁾) KL(μ_j, λ⁽ʲ⁾_j) ≤ 8 j`, and `∑_{j=2}^K 1/j ≥ (3/5) log K` (E6).
- Lemma 19: in natural parameters `y`, `w₀ d(ξ₀, y) + w₁ d(ξ₁, y)` (Bregman divergences of `φ`) is
  minimal at `y = w₀ ξ₀ + w₁ ξ₁`; the alternatives of two-armed best arm identification reduce to
  `λ₀ = λ₁` (LMLExplore `Task/BestArm.lean`, `Mean.lean`); the maximum over `w₀` solves
  `d(ξ₀, y) = d(ξ₁, y)`, i.e. `φ'(y) = (φ(ξ₀) - φ(ξ₁)) / (ξ₀ - ξ₁)`.
- Lemma 20: Lemma 19 and the sub-Gaussian lower bound `KL(x, μ) ≥ (x - μ)² / (2 σ²)` on the rate
  function (`lem:rate_ge_sq_of_subgaussian`); Gaussian case by `expFamilyDiv_gaussianReal`.
- Lemma 9: Lemma 19 for the Bernoulli family `Ber(1/2)` (`ExponentialFamily/Bernoulli.lean`:
  means `(0, 1)`, members `Ber(m)`, divergence `klBerReal`), `φ(ξ) = log (1 + e^ξ)`.
- Theorem 10: `H_𝒞 ≤ H^{sp}` as in Theorem 8 (`lem:isRegular_baiTask_bernoulli`), Corollary 4 at
  `μ(x) = (x(1+x), x)`, `λ⁽¹⁾ = (x/2, x)`, `λ⁽²⁾ = (x(1+x), 1/2)` for `x < 0.36` (E7), Lemma 9, and
  the limit `1 + (1 - 1/(2 log 2) - log(2 log 2)/(2 log 2)) / (log 2 - 1/2) ≈ 1.2228` as `x → 0`
  (`lem:bernoulli_limit`; numerically the bound exceeds `1` from `x ≈ 10⁻⁹`).
